Getting started

Which applies to you?

  • I'm a parent, family member or friend
  • I'm the missionary

If you're a parent, family member or friend

This is the preferred way to start, and it asks nothing of the missionary until the archive already exists.

  1. Forward one of their letters to post@pdayletters.com — as an attachment.
  2. We reply with a link.

    Follow it and sign in. Use any Google or Microsoft account. It does not have to be the one you forwarded the letter from. You will be asked what to call the archive, and then it is created and ready to grow and share.

  3. Ask the missionary to add post@pdayletters.com to their weekly letter.

    To, Cc or Bcc — any of the three works. That is the whole of what they have to do, and they never hear from us again: once the archive exists, their letters and attached photos are simply filed.

  4. Forward the older letters to fill in the archive.

    Even if the missionary is already several months into their mission (or already home), you can still add the letters they sent before the archive was set up. Forward them to post@pdayletters.com and they are added to the archive, too. Send them all at once or a few at a time — either way they are filed by the date each one was originally written, not the date you sent it on.

If you're the missionary

You do not have to set anything up — a parent can do all of the above without you. But if you would rather start it yourself, here's how to do it.

  1. Bcc post@pdayletters.com on your next letter home.

    We will reply to you with a link to set the archive up. Nothing is published until somebody does. You can follow the link yourself and set it up, or forward it to a parent, family member or friend and they'll do it for you.

  2. Follow the link and sign in.

    Use a personal Google or Microsoft account — not your mission one. That address stops working after you come home, and the archive is meant to outlast it. You will be asked what to call the archive, and then it is created and ready to grow and share.

  3. Then add post@pdayletters.com to the Bcc of your weekly letter.

    That is the last thing you have to remember.
