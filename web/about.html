Who Made This?

In July 2026, we sent our first missionary (our third and youngest child) into the mission field. He’s serving in the Mexico City West Mission. To distract myself from the heavy feelings of missing my son, I decided to create this site to help collect and organize the letters and photos he sends home and to help me create a scrapbook-style book from them when he returns.

Having served as a missionary in Paraguay from 1996 to 1998, I appreciated the scrapbook my mother created containing all the things I sent home. In this modern world of email, digital photos, online search, low-cost service hosting, and on-demand printing, this site accomplishes similar goals. Ease of use, privacy, and security, as well as minimal distraction to the actively serving missionary, have been top priorities throughout my development process. I’ve worked in software development for nearly 25 years, and the recent advent of AI agent-assisted development made creating this site a straightforward side project and a welcome distraction from missing my son.

My costs for keeping the site running are low, so I’m pleased to provide it as a free service. If you buy a printed book, a small margin comes back to the site and helps cover those costs. While my original intended audience was my family as we followed my son’s mission, I’m glad if this site benefits others, too!

—A missionary dad
