This gathers every letter in the archive, in order, with the photographs, and lays them out as a hardcover book — a title page, a contents by date, and a chapter for each letter.

Letters you have hidden are left out, exactly as they are for everyone else reading the archive. Nothing is ordered or paid for here: what you get at the end is the book itself, to look at.

No mission is recorded for this archive, so the cover will carry the name and the dates alone. You can add one in settings first if you would rather.
