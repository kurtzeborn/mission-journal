    Invite someone


    One or many — separate them with commas or put each on its own line. Pasting straight out of your contacts or an old email works; names around the addresses are ignored.


    We email them a link. Nothing changes until they follow it, and the account they sign in with is the one that gets access — it does not have to be the address you type here.
