    Your missionary's letters kept safe, in one place, easily searchable, and privately shareable to family and friends.

    How it works

    After you set up your missionary's archive, they simply add post@pdayletters.com to their emails (To:, CC:, or BCC:) and the rest happens automatically. Letters are filed, photos are kept with the letters, and the private archive becomes a searchable resource for friends, family, and the missionary themselves after they return home.

    Getting started

    Reading an archive

    Archives are not public and are not listed anywhere. If someone has added you to one, sign in with the address they invited and go to the link they sent you.

    Cost

    Archives are free to set up and use. There are no subscription fees or hidden costs. This was created as a labor of love by a father of a missionary.

    Sign in

    Letters are only ever shown to people the family has added by name.
