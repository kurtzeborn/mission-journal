    How it works

    Works before they leave or after they are already out.

    1. Forward one letter as an attachment to post@pdayletters.com.
    2. Follow the link in our reply and sign in to create the archive.
    3. Ask the missionary to include post@pdayletters.com on the To, Cc, or Bcc line of future letters.

    From then on, letters and photos are filed automatically. Older letters can be forwarded later. Owners can add pictures from a phone or Google Photos.

    • Private Only invited family can see it.
    • Searchable Find words, dates, and names instantly.
    • Photos saved Kept with the letter they arrived with.
    • Hardcover book Print the archive when you want a book.
    • Calm and simple No ads. No clutter. Just letters.

    If you were invited

    If someone invited you, open the link in that email and sign in with Google or Microsoft. Archives are not public and are not listed anywhere.

    We can email you when new letters arrive. Choose how often (requires sign-in).

    Cost

    The archive is free. No subscription. The only optional purchase is a printed hardcover keepsake containing all letters and photos. This is built and maintained as a labor of love by a father of a missionary.

    \ ˈpē‑ˌdā \ /ˈpiː ˌdeɪ/ also Pday, P Day · short for preparation day

    1. noun Among the full-time missionaries of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints: the one day each week set aside for laundry, shopping, sightseeing, and writing a letter home. Usually Monday.

      “We’ll hear from our missionary again on his next P‑day.”

    2. adjective of or belonging to that day.

      “Her P‑day letter came in this morning.”
