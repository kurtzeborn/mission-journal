    P‑day

    \ ˈpē‑ˌdā \ /ˈpiː ˌdeɪ/ also Pday, P Day · short for preparation day

    1. noun Among the full-time missionaries of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints: the one day each week set aside for laundry, shopping, sightseeing, and writing a letter home. Usually Monday.

      “We’ll hear from our missionary again on his next P‑day.”

    2. adjective of or belonging to that day.

      “Her P‑day letter came in this morning.”

    This site is dedicated to retaining and organizing the letters missionaries send home, keeping them all in one place, easily searchable, and privately shareable with family and friends. All for free.

    Create an archive

    How it works

    After you set up your missionary's archive, they simply add post@pdayletters.com to their emails (To:, CC:, or BCC:) and the rest happens automatically. Letters are filed, photos are kept with the letters, and the private archive becomes a searchable resource for friends, family, and the missionary themselves after they return home. Archive owners can also add pictures to letters that have already arrived, either from their device or from Google Photos.

    Reading an archive

    Archives are not public and are not listed anywhere. If someone has added you to one, sign in with the address they invited and go to the link they sent you.

    As new letters arrive, we can also email you periodically to notify you of new activity. Choose how often you’d like to receive these emails (requires login).

    After your missionary returns home, you can have a hardcover book printed which contains all the letters and photos that were collected in the archive.

    Cost

    These missionary archives are free to set up and use. There are no subscription fees or hidden costs. This was created and is maintained as a labor of love by a father of a missionary.
