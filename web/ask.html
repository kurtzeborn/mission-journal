Ask for the first letter?

We will send one short message. It explains that you are trying to save the letters in one place, asks them to forward one letter to us, and says who asked. There is nothing for them to sign in to and nothing to reply to.

When their letter reaches us, we will email you the link to set the archive up. You stay in charge of it.

Would rather not interrupt them? Open outlook.com in a browser, find the letter, and use More actions (…) > Forward as attachment. Outlook on the web does not change the message, so that works too.
