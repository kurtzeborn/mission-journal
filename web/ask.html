Ask your missionary?

We will send one short message containing a link, and ask them to forward it to you. It says who asked. There is nothing for them to attach, nothing to sign in to and nothing to reply to.

Opening the link they send you sets the archive up under your account. After that you can forward as many letters as you like, from any mail program.

Would rather not interrupt them? Open outlook.com in a browser, find the letter, and use More actions (…) > Forward as attachment. Outlook on the web keeps the part we need, so that works too, and so does the new Outlook for Windows.
